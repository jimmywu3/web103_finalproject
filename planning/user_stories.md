## Draft User Stories

[👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾 Include at least at least 10 user stories in this format:]

1. As an on-campus student, 
I want to:
Find affordable student housing
Active campus activities
so that I can:
Minimize my cost of living, while prioritizing school, and making connections/friends. 

2. New Entry level (Permanent stay possible first time away from home)
As a new entry-level worker, 
I want to:
Find other early folk just like me
Match the tastes of things I already have going on
Find places to study and have fun at
so that I can:
 Adapt to this environment quickly and take this into consideration if I want to live here.

3. Low-income families (Forced to move be )
As someone from a low-income family, 
I want to:
Find the cheapest resources, jobs around and available.
so that I can:
Be as financially comfortable as possible

4. Children of people that move a lot
As a child of  a person that moves a lot, 
I want to:
Quickly make friends with people I can relate with
so that I can:
Enjoy my time while I have it

5. Displaced due to natural disasters
As someone displaced, 
I want to:
Locate come-back programs tailored to victims of displacement
so that I can:
Receive cushioning as I try to get my life back together

6. Introverts
As an introvert
I want to:
Find people with similar interest or hobbies
Meet people in smaller groups or one-on-one
So that I can:
Make friends without being overwhelmed by too many people

7.  Extroverts
As an extrovert
I want to:
Meet new people
Explore new hobbies with new people
So that I can:
Become friends with more people
Always have a community of people I can reach out to

8. Niche hobbyist 
As a niche hobbyist
I want to:
Find others that enjoy the same hobby as I do
So that I can:
Form a small community of people I can reach out to about my hobby

9. Local Event planners
As a local event planner
I want to:
Host events for my community based on certain interests
So that I can:
Form a stronger community in the area I live in

10. Recent Graduate
As a recent graduate 
I want to:
Have a way to make new friends consistently
Meet other recent graduates 
So that i can:
Create a meaningful network
