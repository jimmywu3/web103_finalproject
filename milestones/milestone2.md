## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

We worked on the entity relationship diagram and made progress turning our app’s features into a clear database structure. We identified the main entities, such as attendees, events, and categories, and mapped how they relate. Adding join tables for attendee interests and event RSVPs helped represent the many-to-many relationships.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

One challenge was deciding how to organize the data and represent relationships correctly. For example, attendee interests and event RSVPs involve many-to-many relationships, so I needed to account for those with join tables. It also took some thought to identify which fields should have rules, such as unique emails and RSVP combinations.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

As we move forward, I’d benefit from feedback on whether the database design supports the app’s features and from guidance connecting the ER diagram to the database implementation. We’d also like support troubleshooting any issues that come up while building and testing the app.
